Board

v2 · A · swimlanes
Idea. A 2-D table: each row is a group (task tree, feature, or repo, your pick), and each column is a stage. The eye reads two things at once. Across a row you see how far a group has got. Down a column you see the whole review pile or the whole queue at a glance. The lane header carries a segmented progress bar, so a folded lane still says "2 of 6 landed, 1 flagged". Children sit indented under their parent inside a cell. Ungrouped tasks share one lane at the bottom. On a phone the stage columns scroll sideways while the lane names stay pinned.
