Idea. The simplest change: grouping is a flag (Stacks / Flat), and a group stops being a header with an indented list. Instead it is one card that looks like a stack. The card shows the group's name and its single most urgent member ("now: Tracks layout waits for your review"). It also carries a pip per task in run order (filled = landed or working, hollow = still to come). The board is sorted by what needs you, not by status bucket. A group sits in the section of its most urgent member, so a group never splits across sections. Click a stack to unfold it in place, with its subtasks on a single coloured rail. Flat mode is the same list with a coloured dot and the group name on each row.
